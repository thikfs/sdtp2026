# Product statement

## Client
Who we are building for (role, organisation type, how we reached them). No real names of private persons.
The client is a dentist and his assistant, they work in a small clinic, and we reached them by messaging their phone number.

## The process today
What the person does now, step by step. Where it hurts. How long it takes, what it costs, how often it goes wrong.
Our client currently relies on notebooks to store client data, appointments, and any information needed. It hurts them because it slows them down when needing to look up client history, appointments have to be checked manually and the notebooks take up space. client data is also lost as notebooks get thrown away. it costs about 5 euros a year for the notebooks.

## Baseline metric
One number that describes the process today: minutes per task, errors per week, euros per month. Source of the number.
about 4 minutes to confirm an appointment.

## The statement
When a dental clinic assistant is confirming or rescheduling a client’s appointment but the schedule is on a notebook that the dentist can’t see outside the office, so work stops for verbal confirmation, our shared calendar lets them confirm or move a slot in one action and shows it instantly on the dentist’s phone with a WhatsApp button to the client, so that appointment confirmations go from 4 minutes today to less than 1.

## Out of scope
Three things we will not build, so the agent does not build them either.
1. payment method
2. automated appointment acceptance
3. medical supply and equipment tracking
